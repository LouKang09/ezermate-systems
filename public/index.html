MORE IN THE PLANS

What powers your day-to-day?

Pro covers checkout, standard reporting, inventory monitoring and replenishment. Business adds inter-branch transfers, specialized reporting and the advanced analytics workspace—including branch, menu, payment, cashier and operational insights.

Explore the features
PRO PLAN Full feature breakdown+

Core POS and operational features documented in the current DonMac POS plan comparison:

  • Sales reports, discounts, payment method breakdown and transaction records
  • Ingredient stock monitoring, replenishment and inventory movement audit
  • Products, ingredients, recipe management and recipe costing
  • Standard dashboard with hourly and custom date-range sales trends
  • Transaction and expense ledgers and standard sales reporting
  • One included branch; up to two more at ₱799 per additional branch at renewal
  • Up to 12 months of online historical sales access, with export options
  • Dashboard insight widgets, where sufficient transaction data exists: frequently bought together, add-on conversion, sales growth, opportunity hours, product pairings and daily insights
BUSINESS PLAN Full feature breakdown+

Business includes Pro features and these additional capabilities documented in DonMac POS:

  • Inter-branch ingredient transfers with recorded stock movement history
  • No Pro three-branch cap; additional branches priced at ₱899 per branch at renewal
  • Six-section Advanced Analytics workspace using saved franchise records
  • Store Performance dashboard and 12-month sales trend comparisons
  • Sales versus expenses, menu mix, payment method and cashier performance analysis
  • Operational trend and alert views based on available transaction and stock records
  • Up to 24 months of online historical sales access, with export options
  • Additional branch insight widgets, subject to available data, including branch health, same-store sales growth and product comparisons

Features shown describe the current Pro/Business software scope, not a promise that every report will contain data before transactions or inventory movements are recorded. Free limits and Enterprise scope will be finalized separately.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you customize the system for my business?+

Yes. We begin by understanding your products, services, team, and existing workflow, then agree on a practical scope for your business.

What does an EzerMate system cost?+

Pricing depends on the features, number of branches, integrations, device compatibility, and support needs. Contact us for a tailored quotation.

Can my team use desktop computers or tablets?+

Browser-based systems can be designed for compatible desktop and tablet devices. We assess your actual hardware and requirements before confirming support.

Can you help with existing inventory or product data?+

We can review your existing files and discuss data import or migration. The feasibility and timeline depend on the format and quality of those records.

Do you offer more than POS systems?+

Absolutely. EzerMate also develops inventory tools, operational reporting, business websites, and custom automation for repetitive workflows.
